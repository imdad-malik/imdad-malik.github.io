What Does an AI SEO Expert Actually Do?

AI SEO is not a magic button and it is not simply adding the words “AI SEO” to a page. A useful AI search strategy starts with the same fundamentals that make a website strong in ordinary search: crawlable pages, useful information, clear topics, relevant internal links, strong page structure and trustworthy signals.

From there, the strategy can expand into question-focused content, entity relationships, concise explanations, original experience, supporting evidence and content formats that help people get to the answer quickly.

Google’s current documentation says its generative AI search features rely on core Search ranking and quality systems, so established SEO practices remain important. Google also describes AEO and GEO as industry terms rather than a separate replacement for SEO.

That is why I treat AI search as an extension of good search strategy rather than a shortcut around it.

Google AI Overviews, AI Mode and Search Visibility

Search results are becoming more conversational. A person may begin with a broad question, refine it with follow-up questions and compare several options before visiting a website. A strong SEO strategy therefore has to think beyond one keyword and one blue link.

For an American business, useful content can answer the questions that appear before, during and after a purchase decision. For example, a company selling a professional service may need pages explaining what the service is, who needs it, how the process works, what it costs, what alternatives exist, what questions customers should ask and what makes the company different.

Important: no honest SEO consultant can promise that a page will appear in an AI Overview, AI Mode answer, featured result or a specific Google position. Search systems decide which sources to show. The job of SEO is to make the site technically accessible, relevant, useful and clearly understood.

SEO for Different Types of US Businesses

“SEO services USA” covers a huge range of businesses. The strategy should change with the search journey and commercial model.

Local and service businesses

Local companies often need a combination of location relevance, service pages, useful supporting content, business information and a consistent presence across relevant platforms.

SaaS and technology companies

SaaS SEO can connect product-led searches with educational content, comparison queries, use cases, integrations, problem-based searches and commercial pages.

E-commerce and Shopify

E-commerce SEO should make products and categories understandable while also helping shoppers who are still researching. Product information, category architecture, filters, internal links and supporting content all matter.

B2B companies

B2B search journeys are often longer. Content can support several people involved in research, from the person identifying a problem to the person comparing vendors and the person approving a purchase.

From National SEO to State and City Search Intent

A national USA SEO strategy and a local SEO strategy are related, but they are not the same. National SEO can target broad commercial and informational demand across the country. Local SEO focuses more heavily on a defined geographic market.

For example, a national software company may need content around a product category and nationwide customer problems. A local professional service may need pages that explain its services in the cities and regions where it actually operates.

The important part is not producing fifty copies of one page. The important part is building an information architecture that matches the real business footprint and gives each page a clear purpose.

How I Approach an SEO Project

  1. Understand the business. I start with the offer, audience, competitors, market and conversion goal.
  2. Research search demand. I examine keywords, entities, questions, competitors, SERP patterns and search intent.
  3. Audit the website. Technical issues, indexation, architecture, content quality, internal links and page experience are reviewed.
  4. Build the topic structure. Related subjects are grouped into logical pages instead of creating disconnected articles.
  5. Improve important pages. Titles, headings, copy, links, structured data and intent alignment are addressed where appropriate.
  6. Create useful supporting content. Content is built around real questions and decisions rather than keyword repetition.
  7. Measure and refine. Search Console, analytics and relevant SEO tools can be used to understand what is improving and what needs more work.
My rule for content: if removing the keyword would make the sentence better, remove the keyword. People should be able to read the page naturally from beginning to end.

What Does “Top SEO Expert” Really Mean?

Searches such as “top 10 SEO experts,” “best SEO expert,” “SEO experts USA” and “SEO services” are broad. There is no single objective list that can honestly decide who is the top SEO expert for every business.

SEO Services USA: What Should a Business Look For?

A good SEO engagement should begin with a diagnosis. Before changing hundreds of pages, an SEO consultant should understand what is preventing the website from earning more qualified visibility.

  • A clear explanation of the business and target audience.
  • A technical audit connected to actual search problems.
  • Keyword research grouped by intent instead of a raw spreadsheet.
  • A content and internal-linking plan that matches the site's architecture.
  • Transparent priorities instead of a promise that everything must be fixed at once.
  • Measurement that considers qualified traffic and business outcomes, not only rankings.
  • Realistic expectations about how long SEO improvements can take.

GEO commonly means Generative Engine Optimization. It is a term used for efforts to improve how content can be understood and surfaced in generative search experiences. It should not be treated as a replacement for SEO.

What is AEO?

AEO commonly means Answer Engine Optimization. It focuses on making useful answers easy to find, understand and retrieve for question-oriented search experiences.

How to choose the right SEO expert in Pakistan

Start with your SEO problem

Technical, content, local, eCommerce, link building or a full strategy? Define it before choosing.

Ask how they approach search intent

A good strategy looks at what users want to accomplish, not how often a keyword appears.

Review relevant work

Look for examples close to your industry, website type and market.

Understand the reporting process

Ask what is measured: clicks, impressions, rankings, conversions or qualified traffic.

Be careful with guaranteed rankings

Results change with competition and algorithms. A professional explains strategy and risk instead of promising a position.

Choose the right working relationship

A solo consultant suits some projects and an agency suits others. Compare scope, communication and support.

How much does an SEO expert cost in Pakistan?

Prices vary with scope, competition and market. Job ads in Lahore and Karachi list in-house SEO specialists at roughly Rs45,000 to Rs150,000 a month, and some Pakistani providers list hourly rates from under $30 to about $70. These are rough benchmarks, not quotes. Ask for a written scope and reporting plan.

Red flags when hiring an SEO expert

Guaranteed rankings

No one controls Google's results.

No clear process

They cannot explain audits, intent research or reporting.

Vague proof

No relevant work or only screenshots.

Cheap link packages

Large link counts without quality or relevance.

SEO topics worth understanding before hiring

What is technical SEO?

Technical SEO covers the foundations that help search engines crawl, understand and index a website.

What is content SEO?

Content SEO matches useful information to search intent, topic relevance and audience needs.

What is semantic SEO?

Semantic SEO focuses on relationships between concepts, entities and related topics, not only exact keywords.

What is topical authority?

Topical authority grows when a site covers a subject thoroughly and links related pages in a clear structure.

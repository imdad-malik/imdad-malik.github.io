Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about SEO.

Straightforward answers to common questions people ask before starting an SEO project.

What does an SEO expert do?

An SEO expert works on the factors that influence a website's organic search visibility. Depending on the project, this can include technical SEO, search intent, content, internal linking, site structure, keyword research, authority building and performance analysis.

What is AI SEO?

AI SEO refers to adapting SEO and content practices for an evolving search environment that includes AI-assisted and generative search experiences. The foundation remains useful content, technical accessibility, clear information and strong search relevance.

What is semantic SEO?

Semantic SEO focuses on the meaning and relationships between topics, entities and concepts. Instead of relying only on exact keyword matches, it helps a website build clearer topical coverage and connections between related pages.

How long does SEO take?

SEO timelines vary significantly depending on the website, competition, technical condition, content quality, authority, market and search demand. SEO should normally be treated as an ongoing process rather than a guaranteed short-term result.

Does AI SEO require special schema?

There is no special schema that guarantees inclusion in AI-generated search features. The important foundations remain technically accessible pages, useful content, strong site structure, clear information and eligibility for normal search indexing.

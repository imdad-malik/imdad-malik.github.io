Frequently asked questions

SEO questions, answered clearly.

Short answers to common questions about search optimization and AI-oriented discovery.

What does an SEO strategist work on?

An SEO strategist connects technical website health, content relevance, search intent, internal linking and measurement to improve a website’s ability to be discovered for relevant searches.

What are GEO and AEO?

Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) concerns making useful, trustworthy information easier for generative search experiences to interpret and potentially reference. Answer Engine Optimization (AEO) focuses on clearly answering user questions across answer-oriented search experiences.

Can SEO guarantee a first-page ranking or AI Overview placement?

No. Search results depend on many changing factors, and no legitimate provider can guarantee a specific ranking or AI Overview appearance. Work should be measured using actual search and site data.

How can Google Search Console support SEO?

Search Console can reveal queries, pages, impressions, clicks, click-through rate and average position. These reports help identify opportunities, but findings should be interpreted in context and checked over time.

Does adding schema guarantee rich results?

No. Structured data helps describe page entities and relationships. Search engines decide whether eligible enhancements appear, and markup must match visible, accurate page content.

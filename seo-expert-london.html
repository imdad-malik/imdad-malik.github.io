What does an SEO expert for London businesses actually do?

An SEO expert finds out why your website isn't winning searches from London and UK customers, then fixes the technical, content and trust problems behind it.

In practice the work covers six areas:

  • Technical audit: crawling, indexing, canonical tags, redirects, page speed and structured data.
  • Keyword and intent mapping: which searches matter, and which page should answer each one.
  • On-page and content fixes: titles, headings and service pages written for how UK buyers really search.
  • Local SEO: Google Business Profile and location pages, but only for places you genuinely operate from.
  • Authority building: credible mentions and links from sites your customers already trust.
  • Measurement: tracking enquiries and calls, not just rankings.

London adds its own pressure. Competition is dense across law, finance, property, healthcare and software, and many searches name a district, such as the City, Canary Wharf or Shoreditch. A plan built on real search intent beats a generic keyword list in that market.

How do London businesses get cited in AI Overviews, ChatGPT and Perplexity?

Pages get cited when they answer a question clearly and cover the related questions around it. Ranking in the top 10 helps, but it is no longer the only route.

Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) is the practice of making content easy for AI systems to find, understand and cite. Answer Engine Optimization (AEO) focuses on giving clear, direct answers to the questions people ask.

What the data says

  • Ahrefs reports that about 38 percent of pages cited in AI Overviews also rank in the top 10 for the same query, down from about 76 percent in July 2025.
  • The rest come from deeper in the results or outside the top 100. Search Engine Journal explains this through query fan-out, where Google splits one search into related sub-questions.
  • So a page that answers several connected questions well has more ways to be picked up.

Imdad Malik builds pages for this in four ways. Each section opens with a direct answer. Claims carry specific numbers and named sources. The author, role and location are stated plainly. FAQ and service schema mark up the content so machines can read it.

No one can guarantee a citation in an AI Overview, and anyone who does is overselling. The realistic aim is to give your pages the best chance of being chosen.

7 questions to ask before you hire an SEO expert in London

Good answers to these seven questions separate a serious provider from a risky one, whoever you choose.

  • Can you show results for a site like mine? Look for named metrics and timeframes, not screenshots with no context.
  • Who does the work? Ask whether you get the person you meet or a junior team.
  • What will you audit first, and why? A clear answer shows a method.
  • How will you measure success? Enquiries and revenue matter more than rankings alone.
  • Do you guarantee rankings? Treat a yes as a red flag, because no one controls Google.
  • Who owns the accounts? Search Console, analytics and content should stay yours.
  • How do you handle AI search? Ask how they approach GEO and AEO, and what they will not promise.

Frequently asked questions about hiring an SEO expert in London

How much does it cost to hire an SEO expert in London?

UK sources put consultant-led SEO at about £500 to £2,000 a month, with one-off audits around £500 to £3,000, though ranges vary by scope and provider type. Large agencies charge far more, up to £20,000 a month or beyond. Website size, competition, technical condition and the amount of content needed all move the price, so compare written scopes rather than headline fees.

What does an SEO expert actually do?

An SEO expert audits your website, finds why it isn't winning relevant searches, and fixes the technical, content and trust problems behind that. Typical work includes a technical audit, keyword and intent mapping, page rewrites, local SEO, authority building and reporting on enquiries.

Is it better to hire a local London SEO expert or work with someone remote?

Neither is automatically better, so choose on proof, process and communication. A remote consultant works through video calls, shared documents and read access to your Search Console, and can cover UK hours with a few hours of overlap. If you need regular meetings in your office, a London-based provider is the better fit.

Can an SEO expert help with both Google and Bing?

Yes. Most SEO work, such as clean crawling, useful content, structured data and trusted links, helps your site in both. Bing also has its own Webmaster Tools, where you can submit a sitemap and check indexing, so it is worth setting up alongside Google Search Console.

How long does SEO take to work?

Most UK providers say material results take 6 to 12 months. Fixing technical blockers can show movement sooner, but competitive London searches take longer because authority builds slowly. Be cautious of anyone who promises first-page rankings in weeks.

What are GEO and AEO?

GEO means Generative Engine Optimization, and AEO means Answer Engine Optimization. GEO makes content easy for AI systems to find, understand and cite, while AEO focuses on giving clear, direct answers to the questions people ask. Both build on solid traditional SEO rather than replacing it.

Does AI search replace traditional SEO?

No. Ahrefs found that about 38 percent of pages cited in AI Overviews also rank in the top 10, so rankings still matter, but citations increasingly come from deeper in the results as well. Strong technical foundations, clear answers and trustworthy content help in both places.
